Frequently Asked Questions

What's included in the white-label setup? +
You get a fully branded version of DOZ UP with your logo, colors, domain, and custom branding. Your customers will see your brand everywhere - on the website, in the app, in emails, and in support communications.
How do I get paid? +
You can either collect payments directly from your customers (and pay us the wholesale price), or we can handle payments and send you your profit share monthly via PayPal, wire transfer, or crypto.
Is there a minimum commitment? +
The initial setup fee covers your first year. After that, there's no minimum commitment - you only pay for the licenses you sell. If you're not selling, you're not paying.
Can I set my own prices? +
Absolutely! You can price your offering however you want. Many partners charge 2-3x our wholesale price, giving them 70-80% margins on every sale.
What support do you provide? +
We handle all technical support. You can either forward support requests to us (white-labeled) or handle tier 1 support yourself and escalate technical issues. Higher tiers get a dedicated account manager.
