Upload & Share

Beginner 3 min read

Ways to Upload

DOZ UP offers multiple convenient ways to upload your images:

Drag & Drop

Drag files onto the upload zone

Clipboard Paste

Press Ctrl+V to paste

File Browser

Click to browse files

Screenshot Capture

Auto-uploads on capture

How It Works

1

Select Your Image

Drag and drop an image, paste from clipboard (Ctrl+V), or click to browse

2

Automatic Upload

Your image is instantly uploaded to our secure cloud servers

3

Get Your Link

A shareable link is automatically copied to your clipboard

4

Share Anywhere

Paste the link in Slack, Discord, email, or anywhere else

Sharing Options

After uploading, you have several sharing options:

  • Direct Link - Opens the image in full quality
  • Embed Code - HTML code for websites and blogs
  • Markdown - Perfect for GitHub and documentation
  • BBCode - For forum posts

Pro Tip

Links are permanent and never expire. You can also set images to private or delete them from your Gallery.

Supported Formats

DOZ UP supports all common image formats:

  • PNG (with transparency)
  • JPEG / JPG
  • GIF (including animated)
  • WebP
  • BMP
