Screenshot Capture

Beginner 4 min read

Capture Methods Overview

DOZ UP offers multiple ways to capture screenshots. Choose the method that works best for your workflow.

Hotkey Capture (Recommended)

The fastest way to capture - press the hotkey anywhere and instantly select a region.

Default: CtrlShiftS

Region Selection

Click and drag to select any rectangular area of your screen. Perfect for capturing specific content.

Full Screen

Capture your entire screen with a single click. Great for documenting your full workspace.

Window Capture

Click on any window to capture it perfectly, including rounded corners and shadows.

All Keyboard Shortcuts

Action Shortcut
Quick Capture CtrlShiftS
Full Screen CtrlShiftF
Active Window CtrlShiftW
From Clipboard CtrlV (on upload page)

Pro Tip

You can customize all keyboard shortcuts in Settings. Some users prefer using the Print Screen key for quick capture.

After Capture

Once you capture a screenshot, DOZ UP automatically uploads it and copies the shareable link to your clipboard. You can also:

  • Open in Studio to annotate and edit
  • View in your Gallery
  • Save locally to your computer
