Getting Started Guide

Beginner 5 min read

What is DOZ UP?

DOZ UP is a powerful screenshot and image sharing tool designed for developers, designers, and teams. Capture screenshots instantly, annotate them with the built-in Studio editor, and share them with auto-generated links that work across all devices.

Step 1: Install DOZ UP

DOZ UP is available for all major platforms. Choose your preferred method:

1

Desktop App (Windows/Mac/Linux)

Download the desktop app for the best experience with system-wide hotkeys and automatic upload.

2

Web App

Use DOZ UP directly in your browser - no installation required. Perfect for quick uploads.

3

Browser Extension

Capture screenshots of web pages directly from your browser with one click.

Step 2: Your First Screenshot

Once installed, capturing your first screenshot is easy:

1

Use the Hotkey

Press CtrlShiftS to instantly capture your screen

2

Select Area

Click and drag to select the area you want to capture

3

Auto Upload & Share

Your screenshot is automatically uploaded and the link is copied to your clipboard

Pro Tip

You can also paste images directly from your clipboard using CtrlV on the upload page!

Step 3: Share Your Screenshot

After capturing, your screenshot link is automatically copied. Simply paste it anywhere - Slack, Discord, email, or any messaging app. Recipients can view the full-quality image on any device.

What's Next?

Now that you've captured your first screenshot, explore these features:

  • Edit in Studio - Add annotations, arrows, and blur sensitive info
  • Gallery - Browse and manage all your uploads
  • Settings - Customize hotkeys and preferences
