Team Collaboration

Intermediate 6 min read

Collaborate with Your Team

DOZ UP Team features enable seamless collaboration for development teams, design agencies, and organizations of any size. Share screenshots, manage team galleries, and streamline your workflow.

Team Features

Team Workspaces Team Plan

Create dedicated workspaces for your team. Invite members, assign roles, and manage permissions all in one place.

Shared Galleries Team Plan

Upload to shared team galleries that all members can access. Perfect for project documentation and team assets.

Activity Feed Team Plan

See who uploaded what and when. Track team activity and stay updated on the latest screenshots.

Setting Up Your Team

1

Create a Team

Go to Settings > Teams and click "Create Team". Choose a name and optional logo.

2

Invite Members

Send email invitations or share an invite link. Members can join instantly.

3

Set Permissions

Assign roles: Admin (full control), Member (upload & view), or Viewer (view only).

4

Start Collaborating

Upload to team galleries, share links, and work together seamlessly.

Team Roles

  • Admin - Full access, manage members, billing, and settings
  • Member - Upload, edit, and delete own images, view all team images
  • Viewer - View and download team images, cannot upload

Pro Tip

Use team galleries for project-specific screenshots. Create separate galleries for "Bug Reports", "Design Reviews", or "Documentation".
