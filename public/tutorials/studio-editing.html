Studio Editing

Intermediate 8 min read

What is Studio?

DOZ UP Studio is a powerful image editor built directly into the platform. Edit your screenshots with annotations, arrows, text, blur sensitive areas, and apply effects - all without leaving your browser.

Available Tools

Arrow

Point to elements

Rectangle

Highlight areas

Circle

Circle elements

Line

Draw lines

Text

Add labels

Blur

Hide sensitive info

Crop

Trim image

Pen

Freehand draw

How to Use Studio

1

Open in Studio

After uploading, click "Edit in Studio" or open any image from your Gallery

2

Select a Tool

Choose from the toolbar on the left - arrow, rectangle, text, blur, etc.

3

Draw on Your Image

Click and drag to add annotations. Adjust color and size in the options

4

Save and Share

Click Save to update your image. The share link stays the same!

Blurring Sensitive Information

The blur tool is essential for hiding sensitive data like passwords, emails, or personal information before sharing screenshots.

  1. Select the Blur tool from the toolbar
  2. Click and drag over the area you want to blur
  3. Adjust blur intensity if needed
  4. The blurred area is permanent once saved

Pro Tip

Use Ctrl+Z to undo and Ctrl+Y to redo. Your edit history is saved until you close the editor.
